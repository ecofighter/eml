## 1. Goal and completion conditions

- Goal: `par` and `compute` are std code over total children; nothing aborts across a child boundary; a runtime error in a child ends the run. Fork evidence, `child_abort` and the re-raise of aborts leave every layer. All of it in the VM's existing scheduler and in native code's lowering of what it already accepts.
- Completion conditions:
  - G-3: `grep -rn 'child_abort\|ChildAbort\|fork\$\|reperform\$\|fork_ev\|fork_of\|alloc_fork_\|ForkTable\|MissingFork\|reraise' crates/*/src std` → empty.
  - `std/Par.em` declares exactly one extern, `fork_join` (`grep -n 'extern' std/Par.em`), and `par`, `compute` are written as D-PT-1 / D-PT-2 say.
  - The new test `run/par/lin_result_dropped_on_panic` passes under `--debug-heap`; the "expected unchanged" files of section 6 keep their expectations.
  - `eml_rt` passes its gate on PT-7 (Verus with the four fork-evidence functions and their proofs gone; `bench/rt-gate.sh`).
  - `bench/run.sh` rows within +0.5 % (`par_tree` has 15 forks and cannot move); `pfib 30` with cutoff 2 at N = 1 is recorded (D-PT-9) from the new `bench/pfib.em`.

## 2. Scope

- In: D-PT-1..D-PT-9 and the "Removed" list of 01 §6; the VM (`compile/forks.rs`, the fork evidence mirror, the join's and the job's abort ends, class `Stops`, D-PT-7); `eml_rt` (PT-7); `eml_native`'s `Par` / `ChildAbort` forms' abort parts and `cells::JobEnd` reduced to a value.
- Out: placement or claiming of children (`sched-core`, `jit-tasks`); the soft limit (`task-limits`, which deletes D-PT-5's sentence); the data-parallel library (`std-par`).

## 3. Received from earlier stages

- `async-root`: no nested root; tasks start at class `Task` with the whole depth (D-AR-6), so a fork inside a task is the only place a child class differs from its parent's.
- `engine-trust`: `Trusted`; nothing else.

## 4. Decisions (01 §6)

- D-PT-1: `std/Par.em`: `extern fork_join : (Unit -> a) -> (Unit -> b) -> (a, b)` and `par` by named bindings (`(Err m, r) -> panic m`, `(l, Err m) -> panic m`), so a `Lin` result is dropped before the non-returning call; a full application of `fork_join` is the `par` instruction; `both` unchanged.
- D-PT-2: `compute` is std code over the job rows with `fn () -> try_panic f`; `take_job` returns the value; `Err m` is raised by `panic m`; `Panic` is in `compute`'s row.
- D-PT-3: a child's `evv` is `()`; the parent's `evv` and limit class are saved at the fork and restored at the join by one `switch_to`.
- D-PT-4: a child that ends with a yield in flight is `Internal("a par child ends in the middle of a yield")` in every build.
- D-PT-5: children run at class `Stops`; a soft-limit flag seen during a child stays on the cell and is delivered at the parent's next entry after the join.
- D-PT-6: the `par` instruction is not an unwind point.
- D-PT-7: in the VM, a runtime error in a child (run by its parent or as a placed `Task`) writes the run's fatal slot; the join's error record, `task_failed`'s child branch and the orphan mark are deleted; with two failing children at N >= 2 the reported error is the first written, stated in stdlib.md 「`Std.Par`」 and vm.md 「子の誤り」.
- D-PT-8: why `try_panic` makes a child total (stated in stdlib.md).
- D-PT-9: the cost (+0.38 µs per empty fork on the VM) is accepted; the later item "a child result without a handler" is already in the roadmap (「その後の項目」「マルチコア対応」, 「handlerを持たない子の結果」).
- Criteria: G-3, S-9 (no yield in flight at a child boundary), S-20 (one extern in `std/Par`), U-7 (E2020's replacement at `par` / `compute` calls: default "ordinary row mismatch").
