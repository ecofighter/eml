# Appendix 02: the rule ledger of the scheduler

One row per scheduler rule of docs/spec/vm.md, classified against the scheduler in `eml_sched` (the `sched-core` plan's SG-14; 00 §D D-22, X-16). Fates: `kept` (the new scheduler has the rule; "Where" names the test of the `sched-core` plan's section 7.2 with its unit, or the later unit that brings the rule), `changed` (the new scheduler has another rule; "Where" names the clause, X-n of the `sched-core` plan's section 5.2 or a clause of 01 / 00, then the test or the unit), `VM-only` (native code has no such thing; "Where" says why). The VM's copy of every row is deleted at `vm-retire`. Sections are vm.md's; a row of 「`Async` のタスク」 names its subsection. No row per test.
